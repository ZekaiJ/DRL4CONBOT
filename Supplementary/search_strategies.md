## Execution and counting notes

- IEEE branch sum: 1,817; unique within IEEE after branch-level deduplication: 1,731.
- Four-database branch input: 6,255; DOI-or-normalized-title connected-component deduplication: 3,138 unique records.
- Google Scholar was excluded from the formal strategy because its ranked output and bounded export could not be reproduced stably. The earlier pilot remains outside the formal reproducible search package.
- The search strings reproduce the recorded search version. Database interfaces may alter punctuation display. Original exports and interface captures are retained in the research archive and are not distributed in this package.
