# Recovery evidence

Recovery is assessed from events reported at each configuration's highest validation exposure. A monitored variable or an available safeguard alone does not establish detection or response. A response must change execution in relation to the reported condition. Human-mediated responses qualify when the source establishes that relationship.

The assessment of 24 September 2026 identifies detection in 6 of 30 configurations, an execution-changing response in 5, restoration in 2 and resumption in 2. Counting each research lineage once gives 6, 5, 2 and 2 among 21 lineages.

Compared with the earlier assessment, eight detection assignments were changed to partial evidence because the cited passages described ordinary feedback, an unactivated mechanism or another evaluation setting. The trenching response was also changed to partial evidence. Two terminated shoveling trials support detection and response in R023; reported torque-limit aborts support response in R055. The previous assignments remain in the repository history.

R065 and R108 still document all four indicators. They use prescribed contact withdrawal or external monitoring and task logic. Neither establishes learned autonomous repair of construction work.

The reassessment is distinct from the independent 20-configuration exposure/protection review; its agreement statistics do not apply here.
